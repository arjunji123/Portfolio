## Gemini prompt for a Pixar-quality hero.png (reference image: public/arjun-child.png)
Use the attached childhood photo as the only identity reference. Keep the boy's face, black side-swept hair with fringe, big dark brown eyes, thick straight eyebrows, skin tone and age 100% identical. No beard, no glasses, no cap.
Create a premium 3D Pixar-style character. Waist-up, front-facing, relaxed half-smile. One hand raised in a friendly wave, the other hand holding a takeaway coffee cup (white, black lid, cardboard sleeve). Black oversized sweatshirt, red track pants with white side stripes, a white AirPod in one ear. Pure white background, soft studio lighting, smooth skin (not plastic), detailed fabric folds, 4K, no text, no logo, no watermark.
Then remove the background (remove.bg) and save as public/hero.png.
